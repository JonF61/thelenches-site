## Pipeline, Holly, Rimmer and the panel
- Submissions' **Done already** button (1 Nov; decide() action `done`, panel only): the Pending status becomes `done` (nothing publishes, no sorry note, no urgent alert) and Contributions shows "(event, done)" rather than rejected. Items rejected before 1 Nov that were really done still say rejected unless their status cell is changed to `done`.
- **Second link** (2 Nov; Pending columns AX `link2_text`, AY `link2_url`): when an item tells readers to visit/report/book somewhere online that isn't link_url, extraction (extract.js SECOND LINK rule, Opus) sets it, using only an address in the email or, for non-newspaper feeds, the links on the item's own page (`scripts/ingest/pagelinks.js`, one fetch, links only); `keepLink2` drops any address not word for word in what Claude was given. The panel shows it and its Edit form has two boxes for it (stale-checked like other fields). Publish writes `link2` into pipeline.json; site and newsletter show it beside the first link. Test: `scripts/test-link2.js`.
- **Already listed** (3 Nov; `scripts/ingest/duplicates.js`): each emailed or feed item is checked against what the site has or will have (hand-written `sites/lenches/items/` still shown, published pipeline.json items, Pending rows waiting or approved; candidates by same day for events, else shared title words), and Holly (Opus) says whether it's the same thing, what the new email adds, and proposes the existing entry's text/links with those facts (links only if written in the email; official entries' words never change). A match: Pending note "Already listed: …", column `duplicate` (JSON: key `h:events/<file>` / `h:notices/<file>` / `p:<id>`, title, url, source, confidence, sure ≥ 0.8, details, changes), status always pending (never published as new, in any mode). Holly's ack, when sure and the entry is live, says (templates.md `ack_listed` / `ack_listed_line`, wording approved by Jon 3 Nov): "Thanks for sending "{title}". We already have it on the website, so it's all set: {link}"; no clarify questions for it. Panel: "Already listed" badge and banner, **Add details** (decide() action `merge`, panel only, fields as JSON in body → scripts/approve `mergeItem`: rewrites the hand-written file (approval.yml commits and runs build.yml) or the pipeline/waiting row's cells; this item → done), Done already, and no Approve or Trust button: a small "Publish as a new item…" link below the buttons asks first (4 Nov, after the Lenches Club fireworks were approved over the warning). **Sender check** (4 Nov, Jon): `duplicate` also holds `same_sender` (duplicates.js `sameSender`: the new From matches the original Pending row's From, or both are on one Contacts row via replies.js `samePerson`); hand-written entries and missing rows count as different; when false the note adds "sender different to original poster" and the panel shows a Different sender badge and warning. Item feedback type `duplicate` (its own Autonomy kind: proposal taken = approved, Done over proposed details or Add details changed = edited, published as new = rejected). Feed items (rss.js, 3 Nov) are checked the same way (links only from the feed text, the article link or its page's links; no ack), except roadworks alerts (one.network), which publish without Jon. Test: `scripts/test-duplicates.js`; browser check `worker/test/ui-listed.check.mjs`.
- **The organiser's version wins** (15 Nov, Jon, on why he published 4 of the first 5 Already listed matches as new): when the organiser of an event or the owner of a notice sends their own version, it takes precedence over what we gleaned elsewhere (a feed, Facebook, a hand-written entry, someone else's email); the gleaned entry should go, not sit beside it. The Already listed card has **Replace with the organiser's version** (15 Nov; decide() action `replace`, panel only; not offered when the listed entry is official or `same_sender` is true): scripts/approve `replaceItem` checks everything first, then removes the listed entry (a hand-written file is deleted and approval.yml commits it with `git add -A sites/lenches/items` and runs build.yml; a pipeline or waiting Pending row becomes `done` with a note) and approves this item, keeping the listed entry's picture when this item has none (image_url, alt_text, credit cells) and a replaced submission's newsletter and flyer counts. Item feedback outcome `replaced` (not counted for or against Holly). An image showing people still needs the People OK tick. Tests: `scripts/test-replace.js`, `worker/test/admin.test.mjs`; browser check `worker/test/ui-listed.check.mjs`. Note: until 15 Nov the Already listed card, Add details and the missing Approve button had never appeared in the panel (the `duplicate` cell was never written; wide-tabs rule under Hard rules), so those four went out through the ordinary Approve button with only the note to warn him.
- **Now on the website** (3 Nov; `scripts/livenotice`, `live-notice.yml` after every successful site build, plus every 3 hours at :47): emailed items approved/auto since `LIVE_FROM` (2026-11-15 21:30: none were sent before then, see the wide-tabs rule under Hard rules), not yet in a newsletter, whose ack or clarify was sent more than 24 hours earlier (26 Nov, Jon: a more recent ack means no second email; Pending `live_notified_at` = "skipped: acknowledged <time>"; livenotice `RECENT_ACK_HOURS`), get one email on the ack thread (awaiting Jon's Approve since 4 Nov; sent at once in holiday mode) once a fresh copy of their page shows the anchor (checked up to 6 times a minute apart; after 3 days unshown: marked "not shown"): templates.md `live_one` / `live_many` (approved by Jon 3 Nov: "Good news: "{title}" is now on the website: {link}" + "If you don't see it straight away, try refreshing the page."). - **Newsletters in feeds** (25 Nov, Jon, after the Harvington PC November newsletter was written up as "read our newsletter"): a non-newspaper feed item that announces a newsletter, bulletin or magazine (rss.js `isNewsletterItem`) has its PDF read (the item's link if a PDF, else a PDF linked or embedded on its page, pagelinks.js `pdfsFromHtml`) and sent to Claude in newsletter mode (extract.js `DIGEST`): each thing in it becomes its own item (at most 8, linked to the article page, note "From the <source> newsletter (<file>)"), never the newsletter itself. - **Web sign-up** (Growth part 3, double opt-in): the form in the sign-up band and home card (`signup-form.njk`, shown while site.json has `signupUrl`) posts to the Worker's `/s` (`worker/src/signup.js`: honeypot, Turnstile, SIGNUP_LIMITER, 100 a day, one email per address per day), which appends a **Sign-ups** tab row (id, requested, email, status, sent_at, confirmed_at, result; made by the Worker) and dispatches signup.yml with the row id only. `scripts/signup` sends the confirmation (7-day signed `/s?t=` link; GET shows a button, POST confirms) or, if already active, an "already on the list" email; Do Not Send addresses get nothing and the row is `held` with an email to Jon, as is a confirm that `applyListRequest` refuses. Confirming adds them to Subscribers (source "web form"). Read it with /k. **Facebook app** (27 Nov, Jon): its browser sends an Origin that is neither site (57 sign-ups refused 23–25 Nov), so for an unknown Origin the Worker asks Turnstile first (`worker/src/formsite.js` `formRequestEnv`, all four public forms) and a check solved on one of our hostnames picks that site (token used once; anything else refused as before); Form failures' `origin` column (G) records the Origin sent (scheme and host, or "null"). The Facebook app may also send our own host over **http://** (27 Nov: 11 Harvington sign-ups refused as the Lenches'): sites.js `siteFromRequest` and feedback.js `formEnv` match the host whether http or https, with or without www (PR 579). **Robot check needing a tick** (27 Nov, Jon): the sign-up form asks "Please tick the box above…" and scrolls to it when Turnstile wants interaction (`before-interactive-callback`), says "Checking you're not a robot…" after 3 seconds, waits up to a minute (was 15 seconds), and its no-token message offers emailing the site. signup.yml's Run workflow input `action` = `added` (ids comma separated) adds checked `review` rows to Subscribers and sends the fixed "You're on the list" email (approved by Jon, 27 Nov; signup.js `addedEmail`); a row refused by Do Not Send becomes `held` and gets nothing. Test: `worker/test/formsites.test.mjs`. **Failed sign-ups go to Jon** (27 Nov, Jon: any failed sign-up with an address is flagged for him to review asap): every refusal above (and every page report, which since then carries the address typed) with something like an address adds a Sign-ups row `review` (result = why in plain words + browser; signup.js `flagForReview`, `REVIEW_WHY`; never honeypot hits; one per address a day, at most 20 a day; not counted as a try) and dispatches signup.yml action `alert`, which emails Jon at once ("Sign-up didn't go through: please check (Lenches)"; `failedEmail`) unless the address has since gone through (row `reviewed`, nothing sent); the panel's Summary card "Sign-ups that didn't go through" lists them until he taps **Reviewed** (`/admin/api/signup-reviewed`, stale-checked review → reviewed; summary.js `failedSignups` hides ones that later went through). Review rows are wiped at 30 days like any unconfirmed address. Tests: `worker/test/signup.test.mjs`, `scripts/test-signup.js`, `worker/test/summary.test.mjs`.
- **Unsubscribing** (14 Nov, Jon): the unsubscribe link (`scripts/newsletter/unsubscribe.js`) and an unsubscribe email to Holly (`subscriptions.js` `applyListRequest`) add the address to Do Not Send and then delete every Subscribers row for it (`removeFromSubscribers`, google.js `deleteRows`), and Holly emails Jon "Unsubscribed: <address>" each time (to GMAIL_USER, never logged). - **Contacts tab** (`scripts/contacts`, contacts.yml, daily 04:41 UTC over the last 8 days; manual `full` = all mail): one row per actual or potential contributor who writes to website@ (an alias of jon@), with a Claude profile and their Pending contributions. Mail with items from an unsubscribed sender adds no Pending rows and gets no Holly reply: its Log row is `skipped` "Unsubscribed sender: …" and Jon gets one "Not published" email per run (ingest index.js `offListReason`). **Duplicates**: one person with two addresses is one row; Jon types the other addresses (comma separated) in column Q `other_emails` of the row to keep, then deletes the other row. The run counts mail and Pending items from every address on that row, Holly's known_contact finds it from any of them, Delete logs them all, and code never writes the column.
