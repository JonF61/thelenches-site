# Newsletter cartoon: image prompt (house style)

Jon makes each week's picture in ChatGPT with this prompt (first used for 12 Nov 2026; Jon asked
11 Nov for every cartoon to keep the same style). Kryten fills in the **Scene** and **Focus**
paragraphs for the week's gag and keeps everything else word for word. The caption is never in the
picture: it goes in `src/_data/cartoons.json`. The only words allowed in the image are a sign's
exact text, if the gag needs one (or none: then the last line reads "no lettering or other words
anywhere in the image.").

## Template

Create a single-panel British newspaper pocket cartoon in a square format.

Scene: {Where it is, very simply: a recognisable Lenches/Vale setting, with any sign "CLEARLY READING" its exact text.}

{The main visual joke, described plainly.}

{The people (usually one or two older villagers, one speaking with a wry, understated expression) and any dog or animal.}

Art style: Draw in a minimalist British newspaper pocket-cartoon style. Use extremely economical black ink linework, loose and spontaneous pen strokes, simplified character shapes and expressive faces. Avoid intricate detail, realistic anatomy, elaborate scenery or heavy shading. The drawing should feel quick, confident and effortlessly funny, as though captured in a few dozen pen strokes. Use plenty of white space and only the occasional light grey wash.

Keep the {setting} recognisable but very simply suggested. Make the {main feature} the main visual feature, with {the supporting details} providing the gentle humour.

Overall mood: Dry, understated British village humour. Observational, charming and gently amusing rather than exaggerated or slapstick.

Strict requirements: Square composition. No speech bubbles, no captions, no lettering or other words anywhere in the image apart from the exact {sign} text "{SIGN TEXT}".

Create a single-panel British newspaper pocket cartoon in a square format.

Scene: A quiet English country lane winding between gently rolling green hills, with a simple row of hedges and a roadside sign clearly reading "EVESHAM RD".

In the middle of the road, a burst water main sends a tall, elegant fountain of water shooting into the air, almost like an ornamental garden fountain. A large puddle has formed beneath it, in which a duck is happily paddling.

In the foreground stand two older villagers watching the spectacle. A man wearing a flat cap and wax jacket holds a spaniel on a lead. Beside him is a woman wearing a headscarf and wellington boots, speaking to him with a wonderfully wry, understated expression.

Art style: Draw in a minimalist British newspaper pocket-cartoon style. Use extremely economical black ink linework, loose and spontaneous pen strokes, simplified character shapes and expressive faces. Avoid intricate detail, realistic anatomy, elaborate scenery or heavy shading. The drawing should feel quick, confident and effortlessly funny, as though captured in a few dozen pen strokes. Use plenty of white space and only the occasional light grey wash.

Keep the countryside setting recognisable but very simply suggested. Make the water fountain the main visual feature, with the duck and the villagers providing the gentle humour.

Overall mood: Dry, understated British village humour. Observational, charming and gently amusing rather than exaggerated or slapstick.

Strict requirements: Square composition. No speech bubbles, no captions, no lettering or other words anywhere in the image apart from the exact road sign text "EVESHAM RD".
