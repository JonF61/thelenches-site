A warm welcome to Thai Emerald Evesham

 

Our beautiful, air conditioned surroundings feature traditional Thai water features, magnificent hand-carved teak furniture and picture carvings, original Thai decor, an abundance of tropical plants and the most superb Authentic Thai Cuisine

We offer a tantalising A La Carte Menu which includes a variety of exquisite dishes, such as succulent seafood or delicious duck specials; and a wide selection of drinks and sweets

 

Our philosophy is to ensure that we provide you with more than just a place to eat, but a unique experience which embraces all of one’s senses

The Thai Emerald recreates the warm and friendly atmosphere of Thailand, with enthusiastic Thai staff dressed in beautiful silk, who are always delighted to provide a personal, courteous service in the true tradition of Thai culture
 

We are recognised as of the best restaurants in Evesham, Worcestershire
