The Pantry at Rush Farm

Farm Shop | Cafe

The Pantry is our café, farm shop and events space at Rush Farm. Situated in the characterful Stockwood Business Park, The Pantry is a converted stable with exposed brickwork and wooden beams. The farm shop provides an outlet for our organic, biodynamic and pasture fed beef and lamb, as well as stocking a full range of organic pork, chicken, fruit, veg and storecupboard staples. Your one stop shop for all things organic! Serving hot and cold drinks, cakes and hot food and with a focus on local produce, you can even get British grown tea at the Pantry Café. We are a Dog Friendly cafe so please bring along your furry friends!

Our Food and Drink

We are a cosy cafe that provides a range of delicious cakes, breakfast rolls and simple seasonal lunches. Our hot drinks are delicious and make fresh by our friendly baristas. Due to the small size of our cafe and kitchen, if your lunch party is larger than four please contact us and book in advance to make sure we have space. No need to book if you just fancy a coffee and cake!

Farm Shop Produce

Our farm shop is the real deal - a small shop where you can purchase Rush Farm organic produce straight from the farm gate. We source local and organic pork and chicken as well as a good range of seasonal organic veg, bread, eggs and store cupboard staples as well as household goods. You are likely to see members of the farm team passing by in the tractor, and our staff will always be happy to help with any questions you have about the way we farm here.

Fresh Milk and Flour

We work the wonderful Wild Cow Dairy to provide fresh milk to our customers. Organic, anti-biotic free and 'calf-at-foot' this milk is not only delicious and full of nutrition it is as ethical as can be. Raw milk is also available by arrangement. 

​

We also have a small stone grain mill, meaning you can get freshly milled, locally grown flour. This is the start of the Inkberrow local food revolution!

Events Space

The Pantry makes a cosy events space for club meetings or small private parties. With excellent catering options we are able to cover anything from a birthday party to weekly book club. We also work with our neighbours Village Fabrics to provide a classroom space that can be catered from the Pantry.

​

Contact us to find out more about hiring The Pantry, as well as our catering options.
