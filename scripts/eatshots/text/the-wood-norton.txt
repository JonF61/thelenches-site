RANKED BY FORBES AS ONE OF THE TOP 20 HOTELS IN THE COTSWOLDS
Escape to one of the best country house hotels in Evesham, Worcestershire

Combining the grandeur of 19th Century French royal hunting lodge with all the comforts of home, the Wood Norton is your escape from the everyday. Pull up a chair at our contemporary bar or go al fresco on the terrace. Indulge in a sumptuous private dining experience. Lose yourself in our gorgeous formal gardens and soak up the view from, quite possibly, the best terrace in Worcestershire.

Our elegant country house hotel in Evesham, Worcestershire is a short trip from some of the UK’s best-loved vistas. The Cotswolds, the Malvern Hills and Shakespeare country are within easy reach and just waiting to be explored.

Whether you’re looking to relax on a country weekend break, celebrate the wedding of your dreams or inspire creativity at a business event, you’ll find it at the Wood Norton.

Stay

Take the exquisite detail of original 19th Century French interior design, mix in enchanting views over the Worcestershire countryside with friendly, attentive staff and you’ve got the Wood Norton. From grand suites to comfortable doubles, we’ve got a perfect room for your welcoming and relaxing stay.
