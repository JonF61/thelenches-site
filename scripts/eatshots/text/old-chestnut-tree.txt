Welcome To
The Chestnut Tree Inn
Lower Moor, Pershore
A cosy, traditional 16th Century British pub anr restaurant with a fascinating history and a few quirky Tudor features located in Lower Moor, Pershore.
Well known locally for our delicious food, family and dog friendly atmosphere, polite and friendly staff and building characteristics, we serve mouth watering food daily alongside a range of lager, real ale, cider, cocktails, hot drinks and spirits.
Relax beside our log fire in our bar area which also features a jukebox and dartboard or enjoy our separate intimate dining room. Warm welcome guaranteed by our friendly team and lovely locals!
