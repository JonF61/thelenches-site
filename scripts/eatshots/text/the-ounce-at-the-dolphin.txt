WELCOME TO
THE DOLPHIN STEAKHOUSE
BISHAMPTON
GOOD FOOD. GREAT COMPANY.

The Dolphin is a much-loved village pub with a long history at the heart of Bishampton. For generations it has been a place where locals, families and travellers have come together to enjoy good food, a drink and a warm welcome.

We're proud to be breathing new life into this historic building, keeping its character while creating a steakhouse and restaurant that celebrates quality British produce, honest cooking and proper hospitality.

Our philosophy is simple — great ingredients, cooked well, served in a relaxed and friendly environment where everyone feels at home. We want The Dolphin to be more than somewhere to eat and drink; we want it to be part of the community, supporting local suppliers, creating local jobs and bringing people together.

See you soon,
The Dolphin Team
