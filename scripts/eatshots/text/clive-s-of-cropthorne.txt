Delicious Local Food

Established in 2018, Clive’s of Cropthorne, set in the heart of the Worcestershire countryside, is a farm shop, butchery and farm restaurant and cafe.

Homemade Food
RESTAURANT 

Our farm restaurant with its stunning views offers delicious breakfasts & lunches as well as homemade cakes and pastries; not forgetting our great Sunday roasts. We regularly change our menu with seasonal produce from our farm & farm shop. We are open from 9-4 Tues – Sat & 10-3 Sun. We are closed on Mondays.

Home Grown Vegetables
FARM SHOP

Our farm shop works closely with local growers & producers to bring you the freshest produce to our shelves. Seasonally we have our home-grown asparagus, as well as other fruit grown on our other nearby farm. All the apples & pears grown on site are made into our delicious fruit juice, which is on our shelves and in our restaurant.

Locally Sourced Meat
VISIT US 

In our farm butchery we source all our meat from farms within the surrounding 3 counties. We work closely with local farmers to bring the best quality local meat to our shelves. We hand-make all of our own sausages, home cure our bacon and make our own faggots. We also have a large range of homemade pies, sausage rolls and pasties.
