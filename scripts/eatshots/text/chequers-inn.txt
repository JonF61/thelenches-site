WELCOME TO THE CHEQUERS INN

The Chequers Inn is a beautiful old English public house and Inn. Situated in the attractive village of Fladbury, which is set on the banks of the River Avon. The Chequers Inn serves Good English Food using local produce and a traditional Sunday Lunch every Sunday & has 7 Letting rooms & a function room available for hire.
