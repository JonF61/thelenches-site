A WARM WELCOME

The Boot Inn is a family run traditional Country Coaching Inn, parts of which date back to the 13th century. Known locally as “The Boot”, it is a lively establishment, offering good wholesome food along with fine ales and wines. You can be assured of a warm welcome from the friendly staff, whether you are visiting for a drink, lunch, dinner or a short break
