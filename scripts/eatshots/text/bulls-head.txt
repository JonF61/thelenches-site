WELCOME TO THE BULL'S HEAD

The Bulls Head Inn is that perfect little slice of home away from home — the kind of pub you wish you could bottle up and take everywhere with you. From the moment you see its classic red brick front, bay windows, and welcoming old sign, you know you’re stepping into somewhere special.




Inside, it’s just like Cheers — where everyone knows your name and your usual drink is already halfway to the bar before you even sit down. The vibe is warm, easy, and full of character. The laughter spills out like music, the regulars have their usual corners, and the staff treat you like you’ve been coming for years, even if it’s your first visit.




Whether it’s sunny days out in the garden or cozy evenings by the fire, The Bulls Head is the kind of pub where memories are made, stories are shared, and time just seems to slow down. It’s your place, your people, your pub.

BULLS HEAD CAFE

Welcome to The Bulls Head Café

Start your day the right way at The Bulls Head Café in Inkberrow. We serve delicious, freshly prepared food made with quality, locally sourced ingredients wherever possible.

Whether you fancy a hearty full English breakfast, something lighter or simply a freshly brewed coffee, you can expect great food, friendly service and a warm welcome.

We are open every Thursday, Friday, Saturday and Sundayfrom 9am until 1pm. Come along, relax and enjoy proper food in the heart of the village.
