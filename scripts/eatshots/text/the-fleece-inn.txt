A warm welcome to our traditional
dog-friendly country inn.
The quintessential village pub.

A 600-year-old pub still at the heart of village life. Savour proper real ales and award-winning traditional dishes at our centuries-old inn in the heart of Bretforton, on the edge of the Cotswolds.

Settle into the cosy pub with its low beams, three roaring fires, and a heritage interior that tells stories of times gone by.

Prefer the open air? Soak up the peace of the courtyard or tuck into one of our heated horse boxes in the orchard, surrounded by apple trees and birdsong.

If you are planning to eat we recommend booking a table, especially during busy times. Open-air orchard seating is unreserved: first come, first served.
