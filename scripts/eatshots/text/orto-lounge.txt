OUR LOUNGE

Orto Lounge is in a great spot, right on the market square right in the heart of beautiful Evesham. It’s a really gorgeous space with an amazing double height, light and airy space inside and a first floor/mezzanine balcony type affair overlooking the floating clouds of globe lightshades. We serve a fantastic range of food and drinks (including full kids, gluten free and vegan menus) in a really relaxed, welcoming, atmosphere and have lovely big windows (that open right out) and a terrace along the front, just perfect for watching the world and its Morris Men go by.

HOME FROM HOME
An informal, neighbourhood food-led café/bar in Evesham. Open all day for you to enjoy a long, lazy breakfast, brunch and lunch, or relaxed dinner.

Orto is Italian for vegetable garden and Evesham is renowned for market gardening.
